# Social Sheep

## Description

This is a SMALL expansion of Adriano Petrucci's eSheep64 desktop pet, focusing on integrating sheep interaction.



## Additions

* Pet interaction!

  * They can bump into each other, they sleep in flocks, they may piss in flocks too...
* New colour palette!
* Window collisions

  * They usually turn around but can also jump through the sides
* Toss animation and smoother dragging
* Other tweaks
